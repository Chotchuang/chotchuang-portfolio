# Owner Brief — Retail Reward Adoption Visualization

## Why this file matters

The case has a clear analytical question but no public visualization or source artifact. A compact, truthful visual brief would make it a useful supporting case for Analytics & Growth roles.

## What to provide

Send the original visualization, workbook, dataset extract, source links, or assignment file—whatever still exists. Do not recreate figures from memory or invent retailer values. If only partial material remains, say what is missing; the public version can state the limitation clearly.

## Recommended public version

Create a **2-page PDF** or a single clear image plus a one-page methodology note.

| Page | Focus | Skill / impact it proves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | One primary chart comparing reward adoption and benefit generosity by retail industry | Data visualization, comparison, and concise business storytelling |
| 2 | Method, sample definition, source/date, interpretation, and limitations | Analytical rigor and responsible use of data |

## Chart rules

- Use a title that answers the business question, not only names the chart.
- Label axes, units, time period, sample size, and source.
- Use direct labels or a short annotation for the two to three most important comparisons.
- Separate observed values from any inferred or manually classified values.
- Use accessible color contrast and avoid relying on color alone for meaning.

## What the reader should learn

Within 30 seconds, the reader should see that you can turn a broad retailer comparison into a bounded insight: which industries show higher adoption, how benefit generosity differs, and what the analysis cannot establish.

## Wording boundaries

Describe this as a focused visualization assignment. Do not claim that the analysis proves loyalty-program causality, revenue impact, or customer behavior unless the source and method support those conclusions.

## What to send for review

1. Original output or source data/workbook.
2. The source and collection date for each variable.
3. How “adoption” and “benefit generosity” were defined.
4. Any values that are estimates, classifications, or manually researched.

## Definition of a publishable version

The public asset must include the source, definitions, sample scope, and at least one limitation. If the original source cannot be recovered, this case should stay secondary rather than being upgraded with reconstructed data.
