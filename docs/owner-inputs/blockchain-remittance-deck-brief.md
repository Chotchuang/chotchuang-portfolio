# Owner Brief — Blockchain Remittance Strategic Action Plan

## Why this file matters

The project page currently states that a completed Keynote strategy deck exists, but a a visitor cannot inspect it. One clean public deck will lift this case from a topic claim to credible strategy evidence.

## What to provide

Provide the original Keynote, PowerPoint, or PDF. If the deck contains private notes, use the original anyway and identify the slides that must not be made public. The public version can be a selected, sanitized subset.

## Recommended public version

Create a **6–8 slide PDF**, designed as a concise standalone document.

| Slide | Focus | Skill / impact it proves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Title, time period, and one-sentence decision | Clear executive communication |
| 2 | Remittance problem and stakeholder map | Problem framing and market understanding |
| 3 | Current value chain and friction points | Operations and systems thinking |
| 4 | Where blockchain may help—and where it may not | Balanced strategic judgment |
| 5 | Phased adoption plan with decision gates | Roadmapping and risk management |
| 6 | Governance, compliance, partner, and customer risks | Responsible fintech thinking |
| 7 | Measurement plan: cost, speed, error, adoption, compliance | KPI design |
| 8 | Limitations and source note | Credibility and evidence discipline |

## Wording boundaries

Use: “strategy study,” “proposed action plan,” “potential,” “illustrative,” or “assumption” where appropriate.

Do not use: “implemented,” “saved,” “reduced cost,” “improved remittance,” or company-specific performance claims unless the original source proves them.

Do not include confidential company information, personal data, account details, API credentials, or unlicensed third-party research.

## What to send for review

1. The source deck or PDF.
2. A note identifying any confidential slides or claims.
3. The date/time period of the work.
4. A short answer to: “What decision was this plan intended to help make?”

## Definition of a publishable version

The final PDF should make clear that this is a completed strategy study, not a deployed remittance system or validated current unit-economics model.
